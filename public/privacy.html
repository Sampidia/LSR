Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to Lagos Street Race (accessible at lsr.sampidia.com). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your choices regarding your experience.

1. Information We Collect

Lagos Street Race is designed as an arcade browser experience that respects player privacy:

  • Local Storage Data: We store your game preferences (such as selected vehicle, sound settings, time-of-day preferences, and local high score) entirely on your device using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you connect via an external gaming platform.
  • Platform Profiles (Lagos Life): When played through the Lagos Life virtual platform (lagoslife.app), the game accesses your public platform avatar, username, and in-game entitlements through the Vatar Games SDK to facilitate leaderboards and vehicle unlocks.
  • Analytics & Technical Telemetry: Standard web server logs may temporarily collect IP address, browser type, and operating system for DDoS protection and performance optimization.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

When running in standalone web mode outside of certified gaming portals, Lagos Street Race may display advertising to support server infrastructure:

  • Ad Networks: We may utilize third-party ad networks (such as Monetag, Adsterra, and Google) that may use cookies or web beacons to serve contextually relevant ads.
  • Third-Party Cookie Policy: You may control or disable third-party cookies via your browser privacy settings. You may also visit the Digital Advertising Alliance Consumer Choice page to opt out of interest-based advertising.

3. Data Retention & Security

We do not maintain user databases with personal identity, financial credentials, or physical addresses. All in-game purchases on partner platforms are processed directly through platform-secured payment rails.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our game does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us for immediate removal.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or game practices, contact us at sampidia.com.
