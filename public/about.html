About The Project

Lagos Street Race

Lagos, Nigeria • Cyber-Arcade Experience

Lagos Street Race is a 3D high-speed arcade flight-racing experience set along Lagos's iconic Third Mainland Bridge. It marries the raw, unstoppable energy of Lagos street culture with an adrenaline-pumping cyberpunk future.

The Experience

Weave through authentic Danfo yellow buses, heavy container trucks, and luxury Lagos traffic at speeds exceeding 150 km/h. Jump, barrel roll, dive under roadblocks, and collect energy bolts while listening to authentic Lagos voice commentary recorded by local talent.

Vehicles

  • Flying T (Mark IV): The default agile interceptor coupe engineered for nimble lane cuts and precision flying.
  • Flying Danfo (Heavy Ion): The unmistakable yellow king of Lagos roads equipped with dual turbo thrusters and reinforced bumpers.
  • Flying Tortoise (Jet Cruiser): A legendary aerodynamic cruiser with massive boost capacity and high-speed stability.

Platforms & Ecosystem

Lagos Street Race runs seamlessly across desktop browsers, mobile devices, and virtual smartphone ecosystems like Lagos Life (lagoslife.app). Built on cutting-edge Three.js WebGL technology with zero mandatory app downloads.
